## 📖 Overview
**Agri-Oracle** is a hybrid **AI + Web3** platform designed to stabilize agricultural markets. It uses Machine Learning to forecast the prices of essential commodities (Onion, Potato, Pulses) and leverages **Blockchain technology** to create an immutable, tamper-proof record of these predictions.

By combining AI forecasting with the trust layer of Web3, we empower government agencies (like the Dept. of Consumer Affairs) and farmers to make data-driven decisions without fear of data manipulation.

---

## 💡 The Problem
* **Volatility:** Essential food prices in India fluctuate wildly due to weather and supply chain shocks.
* **Opaque Data:** Traditional forecasting models are centralized "black boxes." Stakeholders cannot verify if a prediction was made *before* or *after* an event.
* **Lack of Trust:** Farmers and small traders often distrust centralized advisories.

## 🚀 The Solution
1.  **AI Engine:** Analyzes historical price data, weather patterns, and crop production stats to generate short-term price forecasts.
2.  **On-Chain Verification:** Every prediction generated by the AI is **hashed and stored on the Blockchain** (Polygon Testnet). This proves the prediction was made at a specific time and has not been altered.
3.  **Visual Dashboard:** A trading-terminal style interface for visualizing price trends and "Buy/Sell" signals for buffer stocks.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### **Web3 / Blockchain**
* **Solidity:** Smart Contract for logging prediction hashes.
* **Polygon (Amoy Testnet):** For low-gas, scalable transactions.
* **Ethers.js / Wagmi:** For connecting the frontend to the blockchain.

### **AI / Machine Learning**
* **Python:** Core logic.
* **Pandas & Scikit-Learn:** Data processing and forecasting (Random Forest / LSTM).
* **FastAPI:** Serving the ML model as an API.

### **Frontend**
* **React.js / Next.js:** Interactive UI.
* **Tailwind CSS:** Styling.
* **Recharts:** For visualizing price data graphs.

---

## ⚙️ Architecture
1.  **Data Ingestion:** System pulls data from `data.gov.in` and weather APIs.
2.  **Model Inference:** The ML model predicts the price for the next 7 days.
3.  **Blockchain Commit:** The prediction data is hashed, and the hash is sent to the **PriceOracle** smart contract.
4.  **Frontend Display:** The dashboard fetches the prediction and verifies it against the on-chain hash to ensure integrity.
